I.What we do

A community of pianists who play for and with each other.

YUPC is a community of pianists at Yale College who convene regularly to perform for and with each other. Started in 2017 by Rishi Mirchandani, we set out to bring together the wide variety of piano talent in Yale College through performances, studio classes, sight-reading sessions, and, excitingly, our semesterly masterclass series. We have fun and create lasting memories!
